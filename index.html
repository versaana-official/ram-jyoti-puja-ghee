Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about RamJyoti Puja Ghee, delivery, and purity.

Is RamJyoti Puja Ghee safe for indoor home mandirs? ▼

Yes! RamJyoti is formulated with zero Light Liquid Paraffin (LLP), zero mineral oils, and zero waxes. It burns bright and clean for over 45 minutes without emitting black soot or toxic fumes.

Can RamJyoti Puja Ghee be consumed as food? ▼

No. RamJyoti Puja Ghee is strictly manufactured and packaged for external ritual use only (Burning Medium / Deep Dravya) and is not fit for cooking or edible consumption.

Do you offer bulk supply for temples and trusts? ▼

Yes, we supply bulk quantities (cans up to 5 Litres and wholesale cartons) directly from our manufacturing unit in Rajnandgaon, Chhattisgarh with special pricing for temple trusts.

What is the shipping and delivery timeline? ▼

Orders are processed within 24-48 hours and typically delivered across India within 3 to 7 business days depending on your location.
